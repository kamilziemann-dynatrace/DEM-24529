XHR Triggered in Callback of XHR

The second XHR is created only after the first one completes and its callback runs.

Ready to run.

Add ?autorun=1 to the URL if you want the scenario to start automatically for Synthetic.
Expected order:
  • First XHR starts.
  • First XHR finishes.
